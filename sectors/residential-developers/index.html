Key Services for Residential Developers

Planning Condition Discharge

  • •
    Contaminated Land Phase 1 Desk Studies: Historical land use assessment, contamination risk evaluation, and Phase 2 investigation recommendations
  • •
    Acoustic Noise Impact Assessments: External noise evaluation, internal noise standards compliance, and mitigation design
  • •
    Biodiversity Net Gain (BNG) Plans: Habitat assessment, metric calculations, and enhancement strategy development
  • •
    Flood Risk Assessments: Site-specific flood risk evaluation and sustainable drainage integration
  • •
    SuDS Drainage Strategies: Surface water management design, attenuation sizing, and adoption arrangements

Building Regulations Compliance

  • •
    SAP Calculations & EPC Certificates: Energy performance assessment, Part L compliance, and as-built verification
  • •
    Overheating Assessment (Part O): Dynamic thermal modelling and overheating risk mitigation for new residential buildings
  • •
    Sound Insulation Testing (Part E): Pre-completion and completion testing for airborne and impact sound transmission
  • •
    Air Tightness Testing: ATTMA certified testing for Part L compliance and building performance verification
  • •
    Verification & As-Built Reports: Construction compliance verification and building control submission support
