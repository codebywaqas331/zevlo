Student Accommodation — Planning & Building Regulations Services

Specialized Services for PBSA Developments

Purpose Built Student Accommodation (PBSA) has unique regulatory requirements that differ from standard residential developments. FIMSCO provides specialized expertise in student accommodation compliance, understanding the specific needs of communal living, mixed-use developments, and high-density occupancy.

Key Compliance Areas for Student Housing

Acoustic Performance

  • • Sound insulation between student rooms
  • • Common area noise control
  • • External noise from campus activities
  • • Plant and services noise assessment
  • • Post-completion acoustic testing

Building Standards

  • • Fire safety and means of escape
  • • Accessibility compliance (Part M)
  • • Ventilation for high occupancy
  • • Energy efficiency standards
  • • Student accommodation specific requirements

Student Accommodation Planning Conditions

Campus Integration

Travel plans, parking assessments, and integration with existing campus infrastructure and local communities.

Environmental Impact

Noise impact on surrounding areas, waste management strategies, and biodiversity enhancement for campus developments.

Specialized PBSA Services

Multi-Use Compliance

Expertise in mixed-use developments combining student accommodation with retail, leisure, and educational facilities.

Rapid Delivery

Operational Focus

Compliance solutions that support efficient operation and maintenance of purpose-built student accommodation.
