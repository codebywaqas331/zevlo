Pre-Occupation Checklist

Requirements Before Occupation

This checklist covers all essential requirements that must be completed and approved before any part of a development can be occupied. Ensure compliance with planning conditions and building regulations.

Compliance Requirements

Building Standards

  • • Building control completion certificate
  • • Fire safety certificate if required
  • • Sound insulation testing completed
  • • Air tightness testing completed
  • • Energy Performance Certificate (EPC)

Planning Compliance

  • • All pre-occupation conditions discharged
  • • Landscaping implementation complete
  • • Access and parking arrangements in place
  • • Waste management arrangements confirmed
  • • Travel plan submitted if required
