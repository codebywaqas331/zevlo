    Construction Management Plan (CMP) — Template

    About This Template

    This comprehensive Construction Management Plan template provides a framework for managing construction activities while minimizing impacts on the surrounding environment and community. Suitable for most UK development projects.

    Template Sections

    Site Setup and Welfare

    Site boundaries, welfare facilities, storage areas, and security measures.

    Traffic and Access Management

    Construction vehicle routes, site access arrangements, and parking provisions.

    Environmental Controls

    Dust suppression, noise control, waste management, and pollution prevention measures.
