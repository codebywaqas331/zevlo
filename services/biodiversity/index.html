Biodiversity Net Gain FAQs

Expert answers to common BNG and ecological questions

Is Biodiversity Net Gain mandatory for my development?

Yes, BNG is mandatory for most developments since February 2024. Major developments (10+ homes or 1000+ sqm commercial) and small sites must deliver 10% biodiversity net gain. Very few exemptions exist.

How much does achieving BNG compliance cost?

BNG costs vary by site but typically represent 1-3% of development value. Our strategic approach often reduces costs through on-site solutions while adding 15-25% value through biodiversity enhancement.

Can I achieve BNG through off-site habitat creation?

Yes, off-site delivery is permitted when on-site provision isn't possible. Options include habitat banks, conservation partnerships, or statutory biodiversity credits. Local delivery is preferred and often more cost-effective.

How long does BNG assessment and planning take?

BNG assessment takes 6-12 weeks depending on site complexity and survey requirements. Starting early in the design process allows integration with architecture and saves time and money.
