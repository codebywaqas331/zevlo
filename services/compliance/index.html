Frequently Asked Questions

Expert answers to common environmental compliance questions

What are the penalties for environmental non-compliance?

Environmental fines are unlimited for serious offences, with recent cases reaching £500K+. Directors can face personal prosecution, criminal records, and disqualification. Cleanup costs often exceed £1M with unlimited liability.

Do I need ISO 14001 certification for my business?

Many large customers now require suppliers to have ISO 14001 certification. It's becoming essential for tender participation and demonstrates environmental commitment while reducing insurance premiums and operational costs.

How quickly can you resolve compliance issues?

Emergency compliance support is available 24/7. Critical issues are addressed within hours, with immediate action plans. Complete compliance systems typically take 8-12 weeks depending on business complexity.

What's included in environmental due diligence?

Phase I assessment includes desktop studies and site inspection. Phase II involves intrusive investigation and contamination testing. We provide liability assessment, insurance recommendations, and transaction support.
