Water Management FAQs

Expert answers to common water efficiency and SuDS questions

How much can water efficiency measures save?

Water efficiency typically delivers 40-60% cost savings with 18-month payback periods. Our Manchester hotel chain project achieved £320K annual savings through smart monitoring, greywater recycling, and behavioral programs.

Are SuDS mandatory for new developments?

SuDS are mandatory for most new developments under national planning policy. Local authorities require sustainable drainage that manages surface water runoff and provides flood protection with climate change resilience.

What is involved in flood risk assessment?

Flood risk assessment includes hydraulic modeling, multiple source analysis, and climate change impact evaluation. We provide site-specific flood risk analysis with mitigation strategies for planning approval and insurance support.

How do SuDS systems add value to developments?

SuDS create attractive water features and green spaces that enhance property values. They provide multiple benefits including flood protection, biodiversity enhancement, and improved air quality while reducing infrastructure costs.
